# Prospect call

Read when: the counterpart is evaluating whether to buy from you.

## What to extract

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Pain | the problem in their words, with a quote |
| Who decides | names and roles, how they buy, who else must say yes |
| Stage | the stage from `knowledge/deal-stages.md` this call supports, with the evidence. A good conversation does not move a stage |
| Alternative | what they do today: `do-nothing`, `do-it-yourself`, `other-vendor`, or `/` if nobody said |
| Next step | what, who, the date |

## Sections this variant adds

- `## Qualification`: pain, who decides, buying process, budget only if said, stage with evidence.
- `## Market read`: first line `alternative: {value}`, then what they use today and where it falls short, in their words.
