# Deal stages

Company knowledge: the skill reads this file, it never copies it. Replace the rows with your own.

| Stage | Exit evidence, said or done by the buyer |
|---|---|
| discovery | a pain named in their words, and who decides |
| demo | a demo on their own data booked, the decision maker invited |
| proposal | a proposal requested, with the budget owner named |
| negotiation | terms discussed in writing |
| closed-won | a signed order |
| closed-lost | a written no, or no reply after the agreed date |
