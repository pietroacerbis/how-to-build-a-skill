# Quality bar

Read before the first quote of every run.

| Bad | Good | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "They love the product" | "We open it every morning before standup" (Marta Keller) | a quote is their words, never your summary of them |
| "Pricing is a concern" | "If it's more than the dispatch tool, I can't sign it alone" (Marta Keller) | the exact words carry the constraint: a signing limit |
| `## Decisions`: demo went well | `## Decisions`: / | a feeling is not a decision |
| Action: follow up | Action: send the security answers, Sam, 2026-11-14 | an action has one owner and a date |
| 2,800 words retelling the call | 600 words on what moved | the transcript is already saved next to the summary |
