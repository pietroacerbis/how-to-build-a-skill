# Customer call

Read when: the counterpart already pays you.

## What to extract

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Working | what they use and what it does for them, with a quote |
| Not working | each problem, who hit it, how often |
| Risk | renewal date, a champion leaving, usage going down, a competitor named |
| Requests | each request, who asked, why, how urgent in their words |

## Sections this variant adds

- `## Health`: working, not working, risk. One line each, a quote where there is one.
- `## Requests`: one line per request, `request, who, why, urgency`. Write `/` if none.
