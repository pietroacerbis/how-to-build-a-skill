# market-read, what a call says about the alternatives

A block several variants open: `prospect` always, `customer` when they name an alternative to you.

    alternative: do-nothing | do-it-yourself | other-vendor | /
    vendor:      <the name as they said it, or />
    evidence:    "<verbatim>"

Then two or three lines, in their words where possible: what they use today and where it falls short.

- `/` means we listened and nobody named one. It is never left out.
- A vendor named by us and not by them is not evidence.
