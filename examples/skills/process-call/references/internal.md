# Internal call

Read when: only your own team was in the room.

## What to extract

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Topics | each topic discussed and where it landed |
| Decisions | only what was resolved, with who decided |
| Owners | every action with one owner and a date |
| Open questions | what the room could not answer, and who will find out |

## Sections this variant adds

- `## Topics`: one line per topic, `topic: where it landed`.
- `## Open questions`: one line per question, with the person who will find out.
